Programmes

FIND YOUR FIT

WEEKLY GROUP TRAINING

U4–U16 90 min / week

The core N10 programme — one weekly session that builds real, visible improvement over the season. Your child will train with a group their own age, develop genuine friendships, and come home genuinely proud of what they've learned. Most parents tell us their child's confidence on the ball doubles within the first six weeks.

Key Skills

  • First touch & ball control
  • Passing & movement
  • Shooting technique
  • Team shape & positioning

Benefits

  • Consistent progression
  • Team bonding
  • Age-appropriate coaching

1-TO-1 COACHING

All Ages 60 min / session

If your child has a specific area they want to improve — or if you've noticed them holding back in group sessions — one-to-one coaching gives them undivided attention from Coach Nathan. No distractions, no waiting around. Just 60 focused minutes on the exact thing that will make the biggest difference to their game.

Key Skills

  • Weak foot development
  • Advanced dribbling
  • Decision making
  • Mental game

Benefits

  • Focused, rapid improvement
  • Fully personalised plan
  • Flexible scheduling

HOLIDAY CAMPS

U4–U16 Multi-day

A full day of football your child will talk about all week. Small groups, structured coaching, and plenty of actual match time — so they go home having played the game they love, not just drilled. Safe, supervised, and genuinely fun. Perfect for keeping kids active over half-term while they make real progress.

Key Skills

  • Skill in small spaces
  • Competition preparation
  • Game intelligence
  • Communication

Benefits

  • Intensive development
  • Fun, social atmosphere
  • Structured & safe

TECHNICAL SKILLS

U8+ 60 min / session

Designed for players who already love the game and want to take a specific skill to the next level. Whether it's finishing, dribbling under pressure, or set pieces — these focused sessions give your child repetition, feedback, and rapid improvement in the exact area that matters most to them.

Key Skills

  • Advanced dribbling
  • Finishing & shooting
  • Set piece delivery
  • Under pressure technique

Benefits

  • Targeted skill work
  • High-rep drilling
  • Complements group training

MATCH PREPARATION

U12+ 75 min / session

For players who already compete for a club or school team and want to perform better on the day. These sessions teach your child to read the game, not just play it — so they feel composed, switched on, and ready when it matters most. Parents regularly tell us the change in their child's match-day focus is noticeable within weeks.

Book a Trial

Key Skills

  • Team shape & formation
  • Pressing & transitions
  • Set pieces (attack & defend)
  • Pre-match mental prep

Benefits

  • Match day confidence
  • Tactical awareness
  • Leadership skills

Session Focus Areas

WHAT CAN 1-TO-1 COACHING

Ball Mastery & Close Control

Develop confidence on the ball in tight spaces and under pressure.

Passing, Receiving & First Touch

Clean ball reception, quick distribution and accuracy across distances.

Dribbling & 1v1 Attacking

Beat opponents with speed of thought, body feints and technical skill.

Shooting & Finishing

Composure in front of goal, technique and converting chances consistently.

Defending & Positioning

Reading the game, winning the ball back and holding defensive shape.

Speed, Agility & Movement

Confidence & Mindset

Your Coach

Head Coach & Founder

COACH NATHAN

EMANUEL

Football Coach · Scout · Mentor

Developing Players. Building Character. Inspiring Futures.

Coach Nathan is an FA qualified football coach, scout and mentor with experience across grassroots, academy and semi-professional football. Playing background includes Arsenal and Crystal Palace academy levels, Kentish Town FC and Clapton FC in semi-professional football, and county and district level.

His coaching career spans KEFA Football Academy, Enfield District U13's Boys and Girls, and Middlesex County U13's Boys. Currently active as a scout at Stevenage FC, with prior scouting experience at AFC Wimbledon. A school teacher and mental health care manager by background, Nathan's approach focuses on player development, discipline, confidence and creating positive environments where young players can improve both on and off the pitch.

The N10 Story

WHERE IT ALL
BEGAN

N10 Academy was born from a simple but powerful belief — that every young player in North London deserves access to high-quality football coaching in a safe, positive environment, regardless of their background or ability.

Founded by Coach Nathan and based at Jubilee Park in Edmonton, N10 Academy has grown from a local grassroots initiative into a respected youth football academy serving boys and girls from U6 through to U18. Our name draws inspiration from the legendary number 10 shirt — the creative heartbeat of any great team.

Over the years, we've trained hundreds of young players who have gone on to represent local clubs, district teams, and county programmes. More importantly, we've helped them grow as people — developing confidence, discipline, and a genuine love for the beautiful game.

How We Coach

COACHING PHILOSOPHY

01

Player-Centred Coaching

Every session is designed around the player. We understand that each child learns differently and adapt our approach accordingly.

02

Technical Mastery First

Before tactics, before positions — we build technically sound players. First touch, passing, dribbling and shooting form the foundation.

03

Psychological Strength

We coach the mind as much as the body. Resilience, concentration and positive self-talk are woven into every session.

SAFEGUARDING COMMITMENT

The safety and wellbeing of every child at N10 Academy is our absolute priority. All our coaches are fully DBS checked, hold FA Safeguarding qualifications, and operate in full compliance with FA Child Protection guidelines. We maintain a clear, written Child Protection Policy. Parents are always welcome to observe sessions. Any safeguarding concern is taken seriously and acted upon immediately.

Common Questions

FREQUENTLY ASKED

We welcome boys and girls from U6 all the way through to U18. Each age group has a programme specifically designed for their stage of development, from introduction to football at U6–U7 through to our senior academy at U17–U18.

Your child comes along to a regular session at Jubilee Park free of charge. Coach Nathan and our coaching team will assess their ability and help match them to the right programme and age group. All abilities are welcome — we don't turn anyone away.

Your child will need football boots (or trainers for their first trial), shin pads, and a water bottle. Comfortable sportswear is fine. You don't need to purchase any specific kit for a trial session.

All sessions are held at Jubilee Park, Houndsfield Road, Edmonton, London N9 7RA. The park is easily accessible by public transport and has ample parking nearby.

The first trial session is completely free. After the trial, pricing is discussed on an individual basis depending on the programme chosen. We offer flexible payment options to ensure no child misses out due to cost.

Yes. All N10 Academy coaches hold recognised FA coaching qualifications (Level 1 or Level 2), are fully DBS checked, and have completed FA Safeguarding and First Aid training. We take coach development seriously.

Child safety is our absolute priority. All coaches are DBS checked and Safeguarding trained in line with FA guidelines. We operate a clear Child Protection Policy and maintain a positive, respectful environment at every session. Parents are always welcome to stay and watch.

About N10 Academy

MORE THAN JUST
FOOTBALL

N10 Academy was founded on a simple belief — that every young player deserves a positive, high-quality football environment to grow in. Based at Jubilee Park, Edmonton, we're not just teaching football; we're developing character.

Our FA-qualified coaches are passionate about unlocking every player's potential, on and off the pitch. With structured programmes across all age groups, we provide a clear pathway from grassroots to elite.

OUR FOUR PILLARS

Everything we do at N10 Academy is built around four core values that shape great players and great people.

01

CONFIDENCE

We build self-belief through achievement. Every player leaves our sessions standing taller.

02

DISCIPLINE

Consistency, commitment and respect. Life skills as important as technical ability.

TEAMWORK
